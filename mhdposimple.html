Privacy Policy – MHD PO Simple

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Android app MHD PO Simple (package com.aistudio.mhdpresov.xkqvrp, "the App") handles user information.

Developer Information

Developer: IsensTec
Contact: isenstec@gmail.com

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share any personal information or sensitive user data. The App has no user accounts and no registration.

Location

If you grant the location permission, the App uses your device's approximate or precise location only on your device, to show public transport stops near you and to center the map on your position. Your location is not sent to the developer or to any third party and is not stored after the App is closed. The location permission is optional; the App works without it.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your favourite lines and stops and your app settings are stored only locally on your device. They are deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

Third-Party Services

To show live vehicle positions, timetables and the map, the App downloads public data from the following services. As with any internet request, these services may receive technical information such as your IP address. No personal data is sent to them by the App.

  • mhdpo.sk – live positions of public transport vehicles in Prešov
  • ArcGIS (arcgis.com) – public GTFS timetable data
  • OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org) and CARTO (basemaps.cartocdn.com) – map tiles

The App does not use analytics services, advertising networks, user tracking technologies, or third-party data collection services.

Data Sharing

No personal data is collected; therefore, no personal data is shared with third parties.

Data Retention

Because the App does not collect personal data, no personal data is retained by the developer.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Unofficial App

MHD PO Simple is an unofficial app. It is not operated or endorsed by Dopravný podnik mesta Prešov or the City of Prešov.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page.
